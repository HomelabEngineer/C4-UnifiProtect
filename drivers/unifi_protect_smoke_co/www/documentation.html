UniFi Protect Smoke and CO Alarm

UniFi Protect for Control4 · version 1.0.1 · Open Source (MIT). Not affiliated with Ubiquiti Inc. or Snap One LLC.
Life safety: this driver is a convenience integration. It is not a listed fire / CO signalling path and does not replace monitored alarm equipment.

Setup

  1. Pick the alarm in Protect Device.
  2. Optional: bind Smoke and Carbon Monoxide to the stock Smoke Detector / Carbon Monoxide Detector contact drivers (Contact Closes On Alarm sets polarity).

Programming

EventsSmoke Alarm, CO Alarm, Any Alarm, Alarm Cleared, Alarm Test, Fault, Needs Cleaning, End of Life, Battery Low, Tamper Detected, Tamper Cleared, Sensor Online, Sensor Offline
VariablesSMOKE_ALARM, CO_ALARM, ALARM_ACTIVE, LAST_ALARM, LAST_STATUS_EVENT, ONLINE, BATTERY_PERCENT, BATTERY_LOW, TAMPERED
CommandsReset Alarm State (clears the Control4 side only)

Typical programming: When Any Alarm › turn on lights along the escape route, unlock doors, stop audio, send a notification.

The driver set

DriverPurpose
UniFi Protect (unifi_protect.c4z)Master. Connects to the console, keeps the realtime event streams open and serves all children. Add one per Protect console.
UniFi Protect CameraOne per camera or doorbell. Live video, snapshots, event history and recorded playback, doorbell ring events.
UniFi Protect Entry SensorSuperLink Entry / Recessed Entry. Door contact output, light automation.
UniFi Protect Motion SensorSuperLink Motion, Glass Break and All-In-One. Motion / glass break outputs, light automation.
UniFi Protect Environmental SensorSuperLink Environmental. Temperature and humidity (Comfort tile + value outputs), water leak.
UniFi Protect Smoke CO AlarmSuperLink Smoke and CO Alarm. Smoke / CO outputs and events.
UniFi Protect SirenSuperLink Siren / Siren PoE. Sound, stop and test from programming.

Child drivers find the master automatically when there is exactly one master in the project. With several consoles, bind each child's UniFi Protect connection to the right master. Then pick the device in the child's Protect Device property.

Troubleshooting

  • Child says Waiting for UniFi Protect master driver: add the master, or bind the child's UniFi Protect connection when there is more than one master.
  • No video but snapshots work: make sure Navigators can reach the console on TCP 7447 (RTSP) and try Preferred Stream = Low.
  • Run the master's Print Devices to Lua Output action and set Debug Logging = On to see the traffic in the Lua tab.
